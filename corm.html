Corm Cultural Center

Beirut, Lebanon · 2019B.Arch Year 2 · Cultural Center

A cultural center set below ground in the midst of a small pine forest, one of the only ones in Beirut.

The green slab extends the park and champions sustainability through low-maintenance, water-efficient grass.

The center sits below ground, in contrast to the Art-Deco style of Beit Corm.
